# Club branding

The PNG is an unchanged copy of the actual Blockchain@NTU club logo from:
https://github.com/Blockchain-NTU-SG/academy-handbook/blob/9443a1bfe4a75345975ed21252bf872cc6f8acd0/docs/.vuepress/public/brand/blockchain-ntu-logo-dark.png

Used to identify this club teaching project. The logo is not newly generated artwork and is not relicensed under the code's MIT licence. Branding rights remain with their respective owner.

Official palette anchors come from the same handbook revision's `docs/.vuepress/styles/index.scss`: blue `#37a4e3`, cyan `#9aeaf6`, ink `#2b2d39`. Darker surfaces and purple accents are derived interface choices.
